# E13: analogical bootstrapping of vocabulary alignment

E0 *unresolved* setting (1000 groups, 8000 cases, 2 distractors): each of 12 domains names its first-order relations differently (`bio:inhibits`, `law:inhibits`, …) with no taxonomy. Each round retrieves 5 cross-domain neighbours per case, maps pairs with wildcard matching (local score 0.3), harvests predicate correspondences from mappings with normalized score ≥ 0.4, and aligns mutual-best pairs (evidence ≥ 1) under new canonical predicates. No labels are used; ground truth only scores the alignment.
